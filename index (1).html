Same question, two temperatures, three runs each

Word overlap: for each pair of runs, the number of distinct words used in both answers divided by the number used in either (Jaccard similarity), averaged over the three pairs. Higher means the runs are more alike. Watch names are counted by their exact heading text.

Model, instructions, conversation and length limit are identical; only the temperature differs. Three runs per temperature illustrate the effect but are not a precise measurement, and even at 0 the runs can differ slightly.
